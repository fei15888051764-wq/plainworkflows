In line with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's guidelines on endorsements and testimonials (16 CFR Part 255), here is exactly how Plain Automation earns money.

Some of the links on this website are affiliate links. This means that if you click such a link and then make a purchase or sign up for a paid plan, we may receive a commission from the company concerned.

This costs you nothing extra. The price you pay is exactly the same whether you use our link or visit the company's website directly. In some cases, affiliate arrangements may even give you access to the same public discounts everyone else gets.

Our opinions remain our own

  • We test every tool hands-on before recommending it.

  • Where a tool has weaknesses, we describe them — affiliate relationship or not.

Which companies this applies to

Affiliate links on this site currently relate to the automation tools we review, which may include n8n, Make, and Zapier, among others. Any button or link that takes you to a product we recommend should be assumed to be an affiliate link.

Why we use affiliate links

Affiliate commissions are what allow this site to exist. They pay for the accounts we test with and the time it takes to write and update reviews — so the content can stay free for readers.
