Integrately vs Zapier (2026): The One-Click Upstart vs the Default — Plus the Third Option

This is the comparison people make right after Zapier's renewal email lands: "isn't there something cheaper that does the same thing?" Integrately is the loudest answer — one-click automations, a fraction of the price, live chat support. The short version

Integrately is cheaper and friendlier; Zapier is deeper and more reliable. Same $19.99/mo entry price buys ~2,000-3,000 Integrately tasks vs 750 Zapier tasks — but Integrately counts filters and routers as tasks, narrowing the real gap. Both free plans are traps (Integrately: single-step only; Zapier: two-step max). Integrately wins on one-click setup and 24x5 live chat; Zapier wins on app breadth (7,000+), branching logic and ecosystem maturity. Budget buyers should price the third option — Pabbly Connect — before paying either.

Both free plans are traps (read this before signing up anywhere)

Each company advertises "100 free tasks a month," and each means something different by it:

  • Zapier Free: 100 tasks/mo, but Zaps are essentially capped at two steps — one trigger, one action. Anything real (filter, format, notify) needs a paid plan.
  • Integrately Free: 100 tasks/mo, but single-step only — literally one trigger and one action, with internal steps counting against your 100.

Zapier's free tier is a demo; Integrately's is a shorter demo. If a genuinely usable free plan matters to you, the honest answer is neither — Pabbly's free plan allows full multi-step workflows and counts only action steps (we mapped exactly what fits inside it in the free plan breakdown), and Make's free 1,000 operations is the generous-but-polling-delayed alternative covered in our cheaper-Zapier roundup.

Where Integrately genuinely wins

One-click setup is real. Pick two mainstream apps and odds are the automation you wanted already exists as a prebuilt recipe — authenticate, done. For a non-technical owner who wants "new Shopify order → spreadsheet row" live before lunch, nothing in this price class is faster. Support that answers: 24x5 live chat even for free users, which shames companies ten times its size. And the price floor: for simple, high-volume, straight-line automations, Integrately's effective cost per task is among the lowest on the market.

Where Zapier genuinely wins

Depth. 7,000+ integrations with mature triggers and actions, real branching paths, filters, formatters, a formatter library Integrately can't match, and an ecosystem of experts, templates and documentation built over a decade. When your automation is weird — and business automations always get weird — Zapier's odds of having the exact trigger you need are several times higher. Reliability and monitoring are also a tier up: task history, replay, error notifications, and mature team features. You pay for it — dearly — but for business-critical workflows some teams decide the premium is insurance. Whether that premium is justified at 750 tasks for $19.99 is another question; our migration story covers what happened when we stopped paying it.

The third option we actually run

Full honesty: for our own business, the answer to "Integrately vs Zapier" was neither. We run Pabbly Connect — roughly $14-19/mo for 10,000-12,000 tasks, only action steps counted, a free plan that allows real multi-step workflows, and a lifetime deal that deletes the monthly bill entirely. The trade-offs are real too: a dated interface, email-only support at 24-36 hours, and an app library of 2,000+ that's big but not Zapier-big.
