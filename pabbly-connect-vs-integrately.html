Pabbly Connect vs Integrately (2026): The Two "Cheap Zapiers" Head to Head

Last updated: August 2026 Reading time: about 9 minutes Hands-on tested, no sponsor

If you've shopped for a Zapier alternative for more than ten minutes, you've met these two. Pabbly Connect and Integrately make the same pitch — "Zapier's job at a fraction of Zapier's price" — and comparison sites usually review each against Zapier while never putting them in the same ring. That's a shame, because they're aimed at exactly the same person, and the difference between them comes down to one unglamorous question: what counts as a task?

The disclosure up front, same as always: the Pabbly links here are affiliate links, and we earn nothing from Integrately — no account, no sponsorship, zero. When this article says Integrately wins a category, that sentence makes us no money. Read the verdict with that in mind.

The short version

Integrately wins on speed and support; Pabbly wins on math. Integrately's one-click prebuilt automations and 24x5 live chat make it the fastest onboarding in this price class. But Integrately counts internal steps (filters, routers) as billable tasks, its free plan is single-step only, and there's no lifetime deal. Pabbly counts only action steps, allows full multi-step workflows even on free, and costs less at every volume. Simple mainstream automation you want live in five minutes → Integrately. Anything busy, branched, or long-term → Pabbly.

The task-counting difference (this is the whole ballgame)

Both tools charge by monthly "tasks," and both free plans say "100 tasks," so they look identical. They are not:

  • Pabbly counts action steps only. The trigger is free, and internal utility steps — filters, routers, formatters, delays — don't consume tasks. You pay only when data actually lands in another app.
  • Integrately counts internal steps too. Filters and routers inside your workflow eat into the same monthly allowance as real actions — so a workflow with cleanup logic burns its budget noticeably faster.

Run the same real workflow through both: "new lead → filter out test entries → format the phone number → add to CRM → notify Slack." On Pabbly that's 2 tasks per run. On Integrately the filter and formatter join the bill — 4 tasks per run, double the burn for identical output. If you've read our Pabbly vs Make comparison, you know this song: the counting model matters more than the sticker price, every time.

Where Integrately genuinely wins

One-click setup, and it's not marketing fluff. Integrately's pitch is millions of prebuilt automations: pick your two apps, and the most common workflows between them are already assembled — you authenticate and you're live. For mainstream pairs (Shopify to Sheets, Calendly to CRM, form to email list), setup honestly takes minutes. Pabbly makes you build every step by hand in a linear list that looks a decade old, because it is.

Support that answers. Integrately runs 24x5 live chat — even for free users. Pabbly's support is email-only and typically answers in 24-36 hours, which I called out as a real weakness in my one-month review. When your automation dies on a Tuesday, the difference between a chat window and an email queue is the difference between a hiccup and a lost day.

A friendlier first week. If you're the kind of person who wants the thing working before lunch and never wants to think about it again, Integrately's onboarding is the gentlest in the budget class. Pabbly rewards tinkering; Integrately rewards impatience.

Where Pabbly quietly wins

Volume economics — the entire first section. A free plan that's actually usable — Integrately's free tier is single-step, which makes it a demo; Pabbly's runs real multi-step workflows, as we detailed in the free plan breakdown. Deeper plumbing — unlimited path routers, iterators, an email parser, scheduling, and instant webhook triggers on cheap tiers (and webhooks cover the apps missing from either library). And the lifetime deal — Integrately has no answer to "pay once, automate forever," which matters enormously if your horizon is measured in years. Frequently asked questions

Is Pabbly Connect or Integrately cheaper in 2026? Pabbly, at almost every volume. Integrately starts at $19.99/mo (annual) for ~3,000 tasks and counts internal steps; Pabbly's Standard is ~$14-19/mo for ~10,000-12,000 tasks and counts only actions. Pabbly also has lifetime plans; Integrately doesn't.

What's the main difference between them? Task counting and philosophy. Integrately counts filters and routers as billable tasks but offers one-click prebuilt automations and live chat. Pabbly counts only action steps and offers deeper branching at lower prices, in a dated interface.

Does Integrately have a free plan? Yes — 100 tasks a month, but single-step automations only, which makes it a demo. Pabbly's free plan is also 100 tasks but allows full multi-step workflows and counts only actions.

Is Integrately easier to use? Yes — its prebuilt one-click automation library means mainstream setups take minutes. Pabbly's linear builder takes an afternoon to learn but rewards you with more flexibility.

Which has better support? Integrately, clearly: 24x5 live chat even on free. Pabbly is email-only with 24-36 hour responses — a real trade-off to weigh against its price advantage.
